Studying how AI agents
work together.

Swarm Laboratory is an experimental research platform for investigating multi-agent artificial intelligence, communication, coordination, and autonomous research.

This website provides public information about the project and its data practices. The research application is privately operated and is not a public service.

Research focus

A laboratory for interaction

Communication & coordination

Exploring how agents exchange information, organize activity, and respond to accumulated interaction histories.

Controlled experimentation

Using configurable experiments and recorded trajectories to examine behavior. Research capabilities and outcomes have not been independently validated.

Planned Google integration

Documents as research resources

The application may integrate with Google Drive, Docs, and Sheets for authorized research document operations and experimental artifact management, including reading, creating, editing, organizing, and exporting documents.

Google document access requires authorization. The intended scope is drive.file: app-created files and files explicitly authorized for the application, with agent-level permission controls planned separately. Google integration is planned; authenticated document operations are not currently implemented in Swarm Laboratory.
