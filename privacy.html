Privacy policy

Effective on first publication · Approved November 14, 2026

This policy is effective November 14, 2026. Google integration remains planned; this website does not indicate Google approval or certification.

1. Scope and application status

Swarm Laboratory is a privately operated experimental multi-agent AI research platform. This policy describes current local research records and the intended handling of Google data if the planned integration is implemented and authorized. The public website does not offer Google sign-in, accept document uploads, or run experiments.

2. Google account information

The planned identity-verification flow would use the authorized account's email address, email-verification status, and stable Google account identifier to check that the intended account is connected. Access and refresh tokens would be used internally to call authorized Google APIs, not provided to agents or included in model prompts. This authentication flow and protected credential storage have not yet been implemented in Swarm Laboratory.

3. Authorized files and operations

The intended Google Drive scope is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, limiting access to app-created files and files the user authorizes for the application, rather than blanket access to all Drive content. File names, identifiers, types, revision metadata, and authorized document contents may be processed. Google Docs and Sheets may include text, tables, cell values, and formulas relevant to an approved operation.

Planned operations include reading, creating, editing, organizing, and exporting research documents, and uploading experiment artifacts. Availability will depend on implemented tools and configured permissions. Google authorization defines the application's potential access; it does not itself establish agent-level permissions. The planned document gateway and automated document-permission enforcement are not yet implemented. Automated sharing, deletion, and revision recovery are not implemented.

4. AI processing and permitted use

The research platform can use mock models or external AI model services. Authorized Google document content may be processed by external AI providers for specific research operations, subject to explicit permissions, applicable Google API policies, and provider data-handling requirements. Relevant content may be included in requests to the selected provider and in resulting research records. This is a potential disclosure to that provider, not processing confined to the local device.

Before Google content is transmitted, the provider, purpose, relevant retention and data-handling terms, and any required consent will be disclosed for that operation. Google authorization alone does not authorize an undisclosed transfer. There is no blanket promise of zero provider retention. Providers may be used only where their applicable terms and settings are compatible with these commitments; Google data will not be supplied to services that may use it to train generalized models.

Google user data will not be sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized AI models. Use and transfers will be limited to authorized, clearly disclosed research document operations and permitted purposes under applicable Google policies. The project commits to following the Google API Services User Data Policy, including applicable Limited Use requirements, and the Google Workspace user data and developer policy. This commitment is not a claim of certification or a claim that the planned integration is already operational.

Agent messages and explicit private-reasoning fields can be recorded. These fields are researcher-visible records, not provider-hidden reasoning. Human inspection of Google content or records containing it requires the user's documented affirmative agreement for the specific data, unless an applicable Google policy exception permits access. Public release of such records requires separate authorization and policy review; Google connection consent is not consent to publication.

5. Local storage and security

Existing research runs preserve configuration snapshots, exact model requests and responses, transcripts, explicit private reasoning when enabled, metrics, checkpoints, and archives. These records can contain information supplied to or generated by agents. Research files are not automatically encrypted by the application, and security also depends on the operator's device, account permissions, storage location, and backup or synchronization settings.

The proposed Google credential design uses Windows current-user DPAPI and restrictive filesystem permissions in a dedicated location outside research artifacts. This is a planned control, not an implemented Swarm guarantee. The intended credential boundary excludes public repositories, model requests, transcripts, and archives. No Swarm Google credentials are currently handled by the planned integration. Existing secret checks reduce accidental exposure but do not guarantee detection of every secret.

6. Retention and deletion

Research records are retained in local run directories and generated archives until the operator removes them. No automatic retention expiry or comprehensive deletion workflow has been verified. Copies may exist in backups, synchronized storage, exports, or an external provider's systems. Deleting one copy does not delete those other copies.

Revoking Google access prevents future authorized access but does not erase previously retained research records or delete documents created in Drive. Google files can be managed directly in Google Drive. Users may contact jasper.muehring@gmail.com to request review and removal of retained copies under the operator's control. No automated deletion, fixed response time, or universal erasure guarantee is claimed. The existing research logging system is not authorization to retain Google content indefinitely: retention and removal arrangements compatible with applicable Google policies must be established before Google data is introduced.

7. Revoking Google access

You can review and remove an application's access through Google Account third-party connections. Reconnection would require authorization again. Removing access does not replace managing retained copies as described above.

8. Sharing, sale, and advertising

This static website contains no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, forms, or cookies set by site code. GitHub Pages, when used to host it, processes visitor information under GitHub's privacy statement.

Google user data will not be sold or used for advertising. Sharing with external AI providers is limited as described in section 4. Research artifacts containing Google user data are not made public merely because the project has a public information website.

9. Operator, contact, and policy changes

Operator: Independently operated Swarm Laboratory research project.
Contact: jasper.muehring@gmail.com.

This policy will be updated when implementation or data practices change. Any required notice and consent will be provided before new uses of Google data. The policy takes effect on its first publication date; later revisions will identify their effective date.
